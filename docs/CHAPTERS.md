# Chapters

How the six formats work and how they fit together, for anyone who wants more than the table in the [README](../README.md). Everything starts from one figure: four chapters that build on each other, and two open formats beside them.

## The four chapters

Each figure has twelve teachings, and every teaching runs through the same four chapters, in this order.

| # | Chapter | What happens | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story | A chapter of the figure's life, narrated, with the text alongside | Recorded |
| 2 | Wisdom | You talk the teaching through with the Echo | Live |
| 3 | Prism | The figure works through the same teaching with three other thinkers | Recorded |
| 4 | Quest | A Socratic test of what you took from the teaching | Live |

Receive, explore, connect, prove. The app shows where you are in it as a mastery level, one to four, for every teaching.

### Story

One scene from the life, told in the first person in the figure's Echo voice. A chapter runs about 13 minutes in English and 14 in German. The text sits under the player and moves with the voice, paragraph by paragraph. The twelve chapters follow the life in order, with a short spoken foreword ahead of the first, and you can start anywhere.

Pause a chapter and one line appears under the player: ask the figure about this. Type it or say it. The answer comes back in the same Echo voice and stays inside what you have heard so far, and the chapter picks up a breath before the second you stopped it. A question counts like any other message on the free tier. If the exchange is going somewhere, carry it on as Free Talk.

### Wisdom

One teaching, one conversation. You talk it through with the Echo, typed or spoken, for as long as you like, and the answers come back in text and in the Echo voice.

### Prism

The same teaching, worked through by the figure with three other thinkers, four voices in one recording. They disagree. Twelve prisms per figure, one for every teaching.

### Quest

A Socratic test of what you took from the teaching: the Echo asks, you answer, and it ends with a spoken verdict. A teaching you have earned counts as gathered, and the app keeps that count per figure, twelve to a life.

## Beside the chapters

| Format | What happens | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Free Talk | Open conversation, typed or spoken | Live |
| Council | Four figures on one question: a moderator and three voices, at two depth levels | Recorded |

Free Talk is the open door: ask the figure whatever is on your mind. It is also where an exchange started mid-chapter carries on.

A council is four Echo voices working through one of 55 questions, one of them moderating. Every question has a second, deeper round behind it, which makes 110 recordings per language. Councils are produced in advance and run from beginning to end on their own.

## Live and recorded

The live formats (Wisdom, Quest, Free Talk) run on the free tier, 30 messages a day per device, or on your own OpenRouter key. The recorded formats (Story, Prism, Council) stream from the catalog in the Echo voices, and every recording carries character-level timestamps, which is what keeps the text in step with the voice.
