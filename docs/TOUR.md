# A tour through Agora Cosmica

Everything orbits one figure at a time. Four chapters build on each other (Story, Wisdom, Prism, Quest: receive, explore, connect, prove), and two open formats sit beside them, Free Talk and Council. Here is what it looks like to use the Library, frame by frame, as of September 2026.

## Step in

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/assets/tour/01-landing.webp" alt="The homepage: the portrait of Marcus Aurelius, the tagline, where his path leads, his first chapter, and one Begin button" width="100%" /><br/>
  <em>The homepage leads with one figure and one door: where his path leads, his first chapter, and a Begin button. 30 free messages a day, no account.</em>
</p>

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/assets/tour/02-cinematic.webp" alt="The cinematic intro: a line of standing figures under a night sky, with the question How should we live?" width="100%" /><br/>
  <em>A short cinematic opens the Library. Tap anywhere to skip it.</em>
</p>

## Meet the figure

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/assets/tour/03-figure-intro.webp" alt="The figure gallery with the About sheet open for Marcus Aurelius: his portrait, what you will learn, a Play intro button, and the paragraph that grounds the Echo" width="100%" /><br/>
  <em>Every figure opens with who they were and what you will learn from them. The voice is an AI Echo, grounded in primary works and historical context, and says so.</em>
