# Crisis Protocol

We built a place to talk about hard things: grief, meaning, death, the life you think you want. Sometimes a visitor is not asking about a hard thing. They are in one. This page says what happens then, in plain words, and where the limits are.

## What we are not

Agora Cosmica is not counselling and it is not supervised by a clinician. Nobody reads your conversations, and no person is watching in real time. The numbers below reach a person.

## The helplines

|---|---|---|
| Germany | Telefonseelsorge, 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222 | Any hour, free, anonymous |
| Germany, children and young people | Kinder- und Jugendtelefon, 116 111 | Free |
| Austria | Telefonseelsorge, 142 | Any hour, free |
| Switzerland | Die Dargebotene Hand, 143 | Any hour, free |
| United States | 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988 | Any hour |
| United Kingdom and Ireland | Samaritans, 116 123 | Any hour, free |
| Everywhere else | The International Association for Suicide Prevention lists crisis centres by country at iasp.info | |

The app shows the line for the country your connection comes from first, then the worldwide directory. It never sends your message anywhere to decide that.

## What triggers what

The screen reads what you type, in the browser and again at our edge, before anything reaches the language model. It asks two questions.

**Are you talking about your own safety, right now?** A first-person statement such as "I want to die", "Ich habe Suizidgedanken" or "je veux mourir" stops the conversation and shows the helplines. The figure does not answer. You are welcome back whenever you want.

**Do you sound like you cannot go on?** Sentences such as "I can't do this anymore", "ich kann nicht mehr" or "everyone would be better off without me" are answered. Three rules are forced into the figure's instructions for that reply: warmth before every other rule, a person or a helpline named before anything from its own practice, and never an image of dying in front of you. A banner with the helpline sits above the conversation for the rest of your visit with that figure. You can close it, and it stays closed.

**Is suicide the subject?** A question about what the Stoics thought, a line about Virginia Woolf, a sentence about Schopenhauer's essay: answered like any other question, with one quiet line above the conversation that names the helpline, once per conversation.

This is pattern matching, fullest in English and German, with a smaller set in Spanish and French. It will sometimes be wrong in both directions. A quotation or a role-play line can stop a conversation that did not need stopping. A phrasing we have not seen can pass. When it stops a conversation it should not have, it costs one message and a helpline. We accept that cost.

## What the figures are told

On the free tier these rules are served on every model and every mode when the screen flags distress. If you bring your own key or run the app locally, your messages do not pass through our edge, and this page's screening happens only in your browser. Quoted from the code:

> When the visitor sounds hopeless, unsafe, or as if they would rather not be here, name one person in their life or a helpline they can reach today BEFORE anything from your own practice. Your practices may follow the referral. They never replace it.

> Never put an image of dying or self-harm in front of a visitor in distress. No blade, no water, no fall, no scene of an ending, not even from your own life or death, not as metaphor.

> When the visitor is hurting, warmth comes before every rule above. Stay with what they said, in their words, and never answer pain with a lesson.

## What we log

A category, a timestamp, a hashed connection address, the figure, the mode and the language, kept for ninety days. Never the text you wrote. The full list is in [CONTENT-SAFETY.md](CONTENT-SAFETY.md).

## Age

The site carries the age-de label for sixteen and older and a youth protection officer is named in the Impressum. See [COMPLIANCE.md](COMPLIANCE.md).

## When we get it wrong

Write to the address in the Impressum with the sentence that was stopped or answered wrongly. We read every one within five days and change the patterns when they are wrong.

## Who reviewed this

Nobody outside the project yet. We have asked a crisis organisation to read the wording and the pattern list. When they have, their reading and what we changed because of it will be noted here.
