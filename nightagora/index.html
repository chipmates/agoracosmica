The six constellations of the thirty

Open any name to explore their life and ideas. Every voice here is an AI Echo: an interpretation built from what each person left behind. Not recordings. Not the dead themselves.

Constellation I · Philosophers · five voices, after Cassiopeia

  • Plato · Classical Philosophy · You will learn to examine your own life.
  • Simone de Beauvoir · Existentialist Feminism · You will learn to see how you were made.
  • Marcus Aurelius · Stoicism · You will learn to question your first reaction.
  • Arthur Schopenhauer · Philosophy of Will · You will learn to see through the wanting.
  • Friedrich Nietzsche · Existential Philosophy · You will learn to build your own meaning.

Constellation II · Spiritual Teachers · six voices, after Corona Borealis

  • Laozi · Taoism · You will learn to act without forcing.
  • Meister Eckhart · Christian Mysticism · You will learn to stop clutching what you love.
  • Siddhartha Gautama · Buddhism · You will learn to watch wanting rise and fade.
  • Rumi · Sufi Mysticism · You will learn to let your longing guide you.
  • Hildegard von Bingen · Christian Mysticism · You will learn to notice the life in things.
  • Dōgen Zenji · Zen Buddhism · You will learn to stop chasing the next moment.

Constellation III · Activists and Leaders · four voices, after the Southern Cross

  • Martin Luther King Jr. · Civil Rights & Theology · You will learn to resist without hate.
  • Mohandas Gandhi · Nonviolent Resistance · You will learn to stay willing to be wrong.
  • Harriet Tubman · Liberation & Faith · You will learn to act before fear stops you.
  • Nelson Mandela · Ubuntu & Liberation · You will learn to free yourself from bitterness.

Constellation IV · Artists · four voices, after Lyra

  • Frida Kahlo · Art & Identity · You will learn to look at yourself without flinching.
  • Leonardo da Vinci · Renaissance Polymath · You will learn to train your own eye.
  • William Blake · Visionary Poetry · You will learn to see the chains you forged.
  • Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart · Classical Music · You will learn to find freedom inside the rules.

Constellation V · Writers · six voices, after Cygnus

  • William Shakespeare · Renaissance Drama · You will learn to see a person from inside.
  • Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · German Classicism · You will learn to look until you understand.
  • Jane Austen · Literary Realism · You will learn to read what people don't say.
  • Virginia Woolf · Modernist Literature · You will learn to wake inside an ordinary moment.
  • Emily Dickinson · American Poetry · You will learn to tell the truth slant.
  • Maya Angelou · Poetry & Civil Rights · You will learn to find your own voice.

Constellation VI · Scientists and Thinkers · five voices, after Auriga

  • Albert Einstein · Theoretical Physics · You will learn to keep asking why.
  • Ada Lovelace · Mathematics & Computing · You will learn to see what a thing could become.
  • Galileo Galilei · Natural Philosophy · You will learn to test what you are told.
  • Carl Gustav Jung · Depth Psychology · You will learn to meet your own shadow.
  • Joseph Campbell · Comparative Mythology · You will learn to read your own turning points.
